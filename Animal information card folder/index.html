The giant panda (Ailuropoda melanoleuca) is a bear native to southwestern China, recognized by its distinctive black-and-white coat.

Key Facts About Pandas: Diet & Digestion: 99% of their diet is bamboo, yet they digest only about a fifth of it, requiring massive consumption to survive.
Unique Features: They have a special elongated wrist bone that functions as an opposable thumb to grip bamboo stems.
Size: Adults can grow to 3 feet tall at the shoulder and up to 6 feet long, with males weighing up to 160 kg (350 lb).
Habits: They do not hibernate and are good swimmers and tree climbers.

Key Facts About Elephants
Species: There are three species: African Savanna, African Forest, and Asian elephants.
Trunk Skills: Their trunks have roughly 150,000 muscle units, allowing them to drink up to 10 liters of water at once and pick up small objects.
Diet: They are herbivores, consuming about of food daily, including grasses, leaves, and fruit.
Size & Weight: Males can stand up to 3 m tall and weigh up to 6000 KG.

Zebras are African equids (horse family) known for their unique black-and-white striped coats, which act as camouflage and insect repellent.

Unique Stripes: Each zebra has a unique, fingerprint-like stripe pattern.
Species:
Plains Zebra: Most common, found in eastern and southern Africa.
Mountain Zebra: Smaller, found in rocky, arid areas.
Grevy’s Zebra: Largest, with thin, narrow stripes.
